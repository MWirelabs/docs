Do not assume Apache or MIT. Each checkpoint and the Docker image has its own terms.

- **Weights**: see the Hugging Face model card.
- **Licensed image**: contract + runbook. No redistribution of the API wrapper by default.
- **TTS voices**: speaker consent required. A CC weight license does not grant voice cloning rights.
- **Corpora**: community collection terms. Takedown via [access email](/access).

Until a card says otherwise: research and licensed internal use, no public SaaS wrap.

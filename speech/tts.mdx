Mono TTS: **one checkpoint and one speaker per language**, not a 50-voice mega model.

**Languages (same as ASR):** Khasi, Garo, Kokborok, Mizo, Nagamese, Meitei.

| Status | What |
|---|---|
| Demo only | English Kokoro on the Garo `grt-en` path. Not a NE voice |
| Planned | `POST /v1/audio/speech` with `language` + `voice` |
| Required | Voice-talent consent before a language is marked live |

Each live voice gets its own row on [Languages](/languages) when the image ships.

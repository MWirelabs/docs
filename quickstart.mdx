---
title: Quickstart
description: Get a key and make your first call in two minutes.
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Create an account at [console.mwirelabs.com](https://console.mwirelabs.com). Access follows the console's recharge and approval steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an API key">
    In the console, open Your keys, add an optional label and click Create key. You can hold up to 5 active keys.
    <Warning>Keep keys on your server. Never put one in a mobile app or web page.</Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make a call">
    Base URL: `https://console.mwirelabs.com/v1`
    ```bash
    curl https://console.mwirelabs.com/v1/translate \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_KEY" \
      -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
      -d '{"text":"Hello, how are you?","source":"eng","target":"kha"}'
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Conventions, then an endpoint">
    [Conventions](/api/conventions) covers language codes and audio format. Then pick one: [Transcriptions](/api/transcriptions), [Speech](/api/speech), [Translate](/api/translate).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Long recordings">
    For 25 to 60+ minute files, follow [Long audio](/guides/long-audio).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Open model weights are on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/MWirelabs). For an on-prem image, see [On-prem](/on-prem/overview).
